A Better Website Experience

This page demonstrates how thoughtful and careful design can make a website easier to read, understand, navigate, and use.

What Makes a Website Better?

A good website uses clear content, readable text, appropriate colors, useful spacing, and a layout that works on different screen sizes. These design choices help visitors find information and complete tasks that is needed from the website more easily.

Helpful User Experience Principles

  • Keep information organized and easy to find.
  • Use readable fonts and comfortable spacing.
  • Choose colors with a strong contrast.
  • Design pages that work on phones and desktops.
  • Gives users clear instructions and feedback on how to navigate the website.
  • Make the website accessible to all visitors
